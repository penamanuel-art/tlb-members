STRAIGHT TALK.

Frequently asked questions

Is a Platinum play guaranteed?

No. No bet is guaranteed. Platinum identifies the day's highest estimated value play, not a sure thing.

What does my membership include?

Access to Sharp Free plays, verified history and educational content. The top Platinum play unlocks with the weekly plan.

Why might there be a day with no plays?

Because forcing volume destroys edge. If the market doesn't offer enough value, the professional decision is not to bet.

How do I unlock the Platinum play?

Use the "Unlock" button on the Platinum card to activate your weekly access.
