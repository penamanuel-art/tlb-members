Straight answers

The questions everyone asks before they join — answered the same way we grade plays: plainly.

Is the free plan really free?

Yes. Your account and the daily Sharp Picks Free play are free forever — we never ask for a card. Platinum is the paid tier: it starts at $1 for your first 7 days, and only continues at $15.99/week if you choose to stay. Your free plan keeps working either way.

When do the plays come out?

The official card posts every day at 1:00 PM ET, always before the games start. Plays are never added after kickoff, and every result is graded against the final score.

Where do I see the plays?

On your Dashboard — that’s home base. The free card also goes out on the public Telegram channel, Platinum members get the full card on the Platinum channel, and email and push alerts tell you the moment the card is live.

How do I pay for Platinum, and how do I cancel?

Platinum starts at $1 for your first 7 days, then $15.99/week, paid securely through Stripe. Cancel anytime from your account page — two clicks, no phone calls, no retention scripts.

What sports do you cover?

Football and baseball are the core: NFL, college football and MLB. A play only posts when the model’s numbers qualify — some days that means a smaller card, and that discipline is by design.

Who is this for?

Members must be 21+. Betting should be entertainment played with a budget — never income, never money you can’t afford to lose. If you or someone you know has a gambling problem, call or text 1-800-GAMBLER — free, confidential, 24/7.
