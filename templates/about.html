ABOUT US

Built on proof,
not promises.

The Sharp Team is a sports betting program with one simple idea: a play only counts if it was posted before the game, at a real price, with the real ticket to prove it — and it stays in the record whether it wins or loses.

Every day our model compares prices across 12 sportsbooks, measures each line against the market's fair price, and publishes only the plays that clear our edge threshold. No filler, no "locks of the century", no deleted losers.

Our three rules

1 · Posted before the game

Every official play is published on the Dashboard before start time, with the price and the book. If it wasn't posted in time, it doesn't count — for us or against us.

2 · The ticket is the receipt

Published plays carry the real betting ticket. You see exactly what was played, at what price, for how much — not a screenshot cropped after the game.

3 · Every play counts

Wins, losses and pushes all stay in the public record, graded play by play. Losing streaks are part of this game and we show them too. The numbers don't lie — so we don't hide them.

How the program works

Plays are released on the member Dashboard — weekdays at 2:00 PM ET, weekends at 11:00 AM ET — and also arrive by email and Telegram. Stakes are managed in units: 1 unit equals 1% of your bankroll, so the same play is sized right for every member. We cover NFL, NHL, NBA, college football and selected soccer matches.
