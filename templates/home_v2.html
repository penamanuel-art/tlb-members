How much should I bet?

Same edge, two ways to size it. Most members start with TST Units. Switch anytime.

TST Units — the disciplined way

Bet in units. 1 unit = 1% of your bankroll. We size every play by its badge: ★ PLATINUM = 1u, GOLD = 0.6u. Small and steady, exactly how The Sharp Team plays it. Less swing, easy to follow.

{% if bankroll %}

{% endif %}

Kelly $ — the math-optimal way

Bet the exact dollars the math says is best. We use quarter Kelly, the model the sharpest bettors use: it stakes more on bigger edges, less on smaller ones. Higher long-run growth, bigger swings.

Questions, answered

Is sports betting actually profitable?+
It can be — but only with a real edge, strict bankroll management, and the discipline to follow the system every single day. Most bettors lose because they chase, overbet, or follow hunches. We publish every play before game time with a ticket photo and grade it win or loss on our public record ({{ res.ganadas }}-{{ res.perdidas }}, +{{ "%.1f"|format(res.roi) }}% ROI so far). No cherry-picking, ever.
How does The Sharp Team find its plays?+
Our Elo-based model prices every game in the NFL, NCAAF and MLB, and we compare it against Pinnacle's no-vig line plus 2–3 sportsbooks. We only post plays with a real edge, shop for the best price, and stake a flat 1u (2% of bankroll) on Platinum and 0.6u on Gold — never more, never chasing.
Do you really bet every play yourself?+
Yes. Alex places every official play with real money and posts the original ticket photo. If there's no ticket, it's not an official play.
What does the $1 trial include?+
The full Platinum board for a week: the daily Platinum play, the +EV board, line-shopping tools, the AI betting coach and the 30-day masterclass. After the first week it's $23/week — cancel anytime from your account.
What happens if I have a losing week?+
Losing weeks happen — variance is part of betting and we never hide it. What matters is the long run: flat stakes, no chasing, every result on the record. Our rules cap daily risk at 6% of bankroll, so no single week can damage the program.
How do I cancel?+
One click from your account page. No lock-in, no calls, no hassle — and your free account stays free forever.
