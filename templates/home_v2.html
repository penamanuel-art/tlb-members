How much should I bet?

Same edge, two ways to size it. Most members start with TST Units. Switch anytime.

TST Units — the disciplined way

Bet in units. 1 unit = 1% of your bankroll. We size every play by its badge: ★ VIP = 1u, FREE PLAYS = 0.6u. Small and steady, exactly how The Sharp Team plays it. Less swing, easy to follow.

{% if bankroll %}

On your ${{ '%.0f' % bankroll }} bankroll, a 1u play is about ${{ '%.0f' % (bankroll / 100) }}.

{% endif %}

Kelly $ — the math-optimal way

Bet the exact dollars the math says is best. We use quarter Kelly, the model the sharpest bettors use: it stakes more on bigger edges, less on smaller ones. Higher long-run growth, bigger swings.

The process

One system. Three filters.

01

FAIR LINE

02

REAL EDGE

The fair line is compared with the best odds. Research can veto a pick; it never invents value.

03

FIXED STAKE

The stake stays disciplined. Neither losses nor streaks change the amount.
