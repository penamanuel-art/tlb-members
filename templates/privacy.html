Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 15, 2026

The Sharp Team ("we", "us", "our") operates thesharpteam.bet, a sports analytics membership service. This policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and the choices you have.

Information we collect

  • Account information: your name and email address when you register.
  • Phone number (optional): only if you choose to provide it and opt in to SMS alerts during registration.
  • Payment information: subscriptions are processed by Stripe. We do not store your full card number on our servers.
  • Usage data: basic technical information such as pages visited, device type, and cookies needed to keep you signed in and to measure our advertising.

How we use your information

  • To create and manage your account and provide the membership service.
  • To send the alerts and notifications you opted in to (for example, that new analysis is live on your dashboard).
  • To send account and billing messages, such as payment confirmations and subscription notices.
  • To respond to your support requests and improve the service.

SMS / text messaging

If you opt in, The Sharp Team may send you recurring text messages about your account and the availability of new analysis on your dashboard. Message frequency varies. Message and data rates may apply. Reply STOP to cancel at any time, or HELP for help. Consent to receive text messages is not a condition of purchasing any membership.

We do not share, sell, or rent your mobile phone number, SMS opt-in data, or consent information with third parties or affiliates for their marketing or promotional purposes.

How we share information

We share information only with service providers that help us operate the platform — such as payment processing (Stripe), messaging delivery (Twilio), and email delivery — and only as needed for them to perform those services. We may also disclose information when required by law. We do not sell your personal information.

Data security and retention

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures to protect your information. We keep account information while your account is active and delete or anonymize it when it is no longer needed for the purposes described above.

Your choices

  • Opt out of SMS at any time by replying STOP.
  • Opt out of marketing emails using the unsubscribe link in any email.
  • Request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal information by contacting us.

Age requirement

This service is intended for adults 21 years of age or older. We do not knowingly collect information from anyone under 21.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be posted on this page with its effective date.
