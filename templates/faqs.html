Frequently asked questions

Everything members ask us most. Something missing? Contact us — we answer every message.

What do I get with a free account?
Sharp Free plays every day on your member Dashboard, one-tap bet tracking, your personal record (net units, ROI, streak) and the program's full public record. No card is required to start.
What is Platinum?
Platinum is the complete card: every official play with its verified ticket, the +EV Board price-shopping across 12 books, player props, line moves, sharp money tools, the AI assistant and a private Telegram channel. It starts at $1 for the first week, then $15.99 per week. Cancel anytime.
When are the plays posted?
Weekdays at 2:00 PM ET and weekends at 11:00 AM ET, always before the games start. The card stays on your Dashboard until 5:00 AM ET, and plays also arrive by email and Telegram.
Which sports do you cover?
NFL and NHL first, then NBA and college football, plus selected soccer matches — only where our model finds a real edge against the market's fair price. Some days the honest answer is fewer plays; we never force picks to fill a card.
How do units and stakes work?
Stakes are sized in units: 1 unit = 1% of your bankroll. A 1u play on a $500 bankroll is $5; on a $5,000 bankroll it's $50. Your Dashboard shows your personal stake for every play, so sizing is automatic and disciplined.
Do you guarantee winners?
No — and you should run from anyone who does. Losing streaks are a normal part of betting (that's variance). What we guarantee is process: plays posted before the game, real tickets, and a complete public record, wins and losses alike.
Can I see the record before paying?
Yes. The full record and the graded ledger — every official play with its result and ticket — are part of the program's public proof. Create a free account and audit us before you spend a dollar.
How do I cancel?
Anytime, from your account — no lock-in, no cancellation calls. Your access runs to the end of the paid period and your tracking history stays yours.
What if a play's price moves before I bet?
Prices move — that's why Platinum includes line-shopping tools across 12 books and a price coach that tells you if a worse price is still worth playing. Never chase a price blindly; the Dashboard shows the price the play was released at.
