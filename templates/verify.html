How to verify any handicapper — including us.

Anyone can post a winning screenshot. Very few can survive an audit. Before you pay anybody for picks — us included — run these six checks. They take five minutes and they separate documented programs from marketing.

The six checks

1. Posted before the game starts

Plays must carry a timestamp from before start time. A "winner" announced after the final whistle proves nothing.

2. Real tickets you can open

The actual bet slip — book, price, stake, time. Cropped screenshots with no price or no book are decoration, not proof.

3. Every play in the record

Losses included. If the record only shows wins, it's not a record — it's an advertisement. Ask where yesterday's losers are graded.

4. Units and prices stated up front

A serious program sizes plays in units (1 unit = 1% of bankroll) and quotes the real price taken, so results can be reproduced and compared.

5. An auditable ledger

You should be able to go play by play — date, matchup, price, result, ticket — and add it up yourself. If the math is hidden, so is the truth.

6. Honest about variance

Every real bettor loses 4–5 in a row sometimes. A program that admits that — and never guarantees profit — is telling you the truth about this business.

Walk away if you see…

🚩 "Lock of the century", every week

No play is a lock. Certainty is the oldest sales trick in this industry.

🚩 Records with no losers

Deleted losses and "private" plays that only appear when they win are how fake records are built.

🚩 Pressure to pay right now

Countdown timers and DM pressure exist so you don't have time to run the six checks above.
