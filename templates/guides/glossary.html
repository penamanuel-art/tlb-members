BETTING GUIDES

Betting Glossary

Every term you'll see on the card, in plain English. No jargon without an explanation.

BCEFGHILMNPRSTUVW

B

Bankroll

The total money set aside exclusively for betting. Your unit size is a percentage of it — 1u = 1% of bankroll — so every member risks proportionally, whatever their bankroll.

Bankroll management

The system of sizing every bet as a fixed fraction of bankroll. Rule #1: the stake is dictated by the system, never by emotion — not in a streak, not after a loss.

C

CLV · closing line value

How the price you took compares to the final price before the game starts. Consistently beating the close is the strongest predictor of long-term profit — our north-star metric, ahead of win rate.

E

Edge

The gap between the true probability of an outcome and the probability implied by the price. Positive edge means the bet is mathematically profitable over time. No edge, no play.

F

Fair price

The price a bet should be, based on the true probability with the book's commission removed. Our model computes a fair price for every game before the market is consulted.

Favorite

The side expected to win, shown with negative American odds (-150). You risk more than you can win — the market is charging you for the higher probability.

G

Grading

Settling a play as a win or loss against the final score. Every play is graded — wins and losses stay on the record, nothing hidden, nothing edited after the fact.

H

Handle

The total amount of money wagered on a game or market. Books watch the handle; sharp money often hides inside it.

Hedging

Placing a bet on the opposite side to lock in profit or cut a loss. Our system rarely hedges: the stake was correct when it was placed, and second-guessing it is emotion, not math.

I

Implied probability

The win probability baked into a price. -110 implies 52.4%; +150 implies 40%. The whole game is comparing this number against the true probability.

L

Line shopping

Comparing prices across sportsbooks and taking the best one. A better price is a bigger edge — the price is part of the bet.

M

Middling

Betting both sides of the same game at different numbers, hoping the final score lands in between. A situational tactic — not part of our system.

Moneyline

A bet on who wins the game, with no point spread. The foundation of our card — most official plays are moneylines. Full guide →

N

No-vig

A price with the book's commission stripped out, revealing the market's honest probability. Our edges are always measured against the no-vig baseline.

P

Parlay

Combining multiple bets into one ticket — every leg must win. The house edge multiplies with each leg, which is why professionals avoid them. Our daily bonus parlay is a fixed $5, outside the official record.

Public betting

Where the casual money is going. We bet with the numbers, never with the crowd — and we never confuse popularity with probability.

Push

A tie against the spread or total — e.g., favorite -3 winning by exactly 3. Nobody wins; the stake is returned.

R

Reverse line movement

When the line moves against the side taking most of the bets — a classic sign that respected (sharp) money is on the other side.

ROI · return on investment

Profit divided by total amount risked, as a percentage. The honest measure of a system — it accounts for prices, not just wins.

S

Sharp money

Wagers from professional, respected bettors — the money that moves lines. We track where it goes, but we follow our model, not the steam.

Spread

A point handicap. The favorite must win by more than the spread; the underdog can lose by less — or win outright. Full guide →

Stake

The amount risked on a play. Ours is set by the system in units — never raised after wins, never chased after losses.

Staking

The method for sizing bets. Ours: 1u = 1% of bankroll; Platinum plays = 1u, Gold = 0.6u. Fixed, automatic, emotion-free.

Steam move

A sudden, sharp line move across the betting market, usually triggered by respected money hitting the same side at multiple books.

T

Ticket

The original bet receipt photo. Every official play carries one — it's the proof the bet was placed, at that price, before the game. No ticket, no official play.

Total · over/under

A bet on the combined score of both teams versus the posted number. Our official card doesn't publish totals. Full guide →

U

Underdog

The side expected to lose, shown with positive American odds (+150). Less likely to win — but often where the value lives.

Unit

The standard bet size: 1% of bankroll. Units keep every member's risk proportional — a 1u play means the same risk for a $1,000 bankroll as for a $10,000 one.

V

Vig · juice, vigorish

The sportsbook's commission, built into the price. Both sides at -110 means you're paying about 4.8% just to play. Full guide →

W

Win rate

The percentage of settled plays won. A secondary metric — a 45% win rate can be excellent at the right prices. CLV and ROI tell the real story.
