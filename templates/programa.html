WHERE OUR PLAYS COME FROM

The fair question every member asks — answered straight.

01 / THE SCAN

EVERY GAME, EVERY DAY

Our model rates every team from real game data — scoring, pace, home and road form, injuries and schedule. Nothing is picked by hand, by hype, or by who is popular.

02 / THE PROBABILITY

A NUMBER, NOT A FEELING

For each game, the model calculates each team's true win probability. Probability always comes first — before we ever look at the price.

03 / THE PRICE CHECK

THE MARKET HAS TO BE WRONG

We compare the model's probability against the price at every sportsbook. A play qualifies only when the price is off in our favor — that edge is printed on every card, in WHY WE LIKE IT.

04 / THE TICKET

PUBLISHED, THEN GRADED

Qualifying plays are posted before game time with the real ticket. Win or lose, the result goes on the public record — and never comes off.

No tips from a guy. No "locks". A model, a price check, and a record anyone can audit.
