Questions

Fair questions

Is it really free?

Yes. Gold plays are free forever, and your first month of Platinum is free when you register — no card, no charge. We would rather prove the work first and let you decide.

When are the plays posted?

On your Dashboard at 2:00 PM ET on weekdays and 11:00 AM ET on weekends — always before game time, always with the real bet slip.

Do you post the losers too?

Every single one. Each official play is graded the next morning, win or lose, and stays on the public record. That is the entire point of The Sharp Team.

What happens when my free month ends?

Platinum closes and your free Gold plays continue. Nothing is charged — we never took your card. If you wish to keep Platinum, it is $15.99 a week, and you may cancel at any time.
