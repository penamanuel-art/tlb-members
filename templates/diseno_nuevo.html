How it works

01
We price every game two waysOur own ratings model (Elo) and the market's no-vig line. A play only exists when the two disagree in our favor.
02
The gates decideEdge, real expected value and win probability must clear the bar for its tier — price never dearer than −160, at any level.
03
Alex approves the cardNothing is official until Alex places it himself and sends the real ticket.
04
The ticket goes on the cardThe play posts with its real price, its real stake and the original ticket photo — before game time, always.
05
Every play is graded in the openWin or lose, it lands in the full record and counts. Nothing is deleted, nothing is edited after the fact.
