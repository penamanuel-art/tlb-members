THE PROCESS

ONE SYSTEM.
THREE FILTERS.

Every published pick must pass the model, the market, and the staking rules.

01 / MODEL

FAIR LINE

Estimated probability is calculated before deciding whether the available price is worth a bet. Team ratings are refreshed every morning, and every game gets its own number before any price is looked at.

02 / MARKET

REAL EDGE

The fair line is compared with the best odds. Research can veto a pick; it never invents value. A play only qualifies when its edge clears the league's minimum — otherwise it is dropped, no matter how good it looks.

03 / DISCIPLINE

FIXED STAKE

The stake stays disciplined. Neither losses nor streaks change the amount. Platinum plays are $100 and Gold and Free plays are $60 — the same amounts every single day.
